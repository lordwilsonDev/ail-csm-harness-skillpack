# Skill 11 — Bias, Confounding & Alternative Explanation Audit
## Goal
Identify systematic error and hidden dependencies.

## Procedure
- Consider selection, information/recall, measurement/misclassification, survivorship, and reporting bias as relevant.
- Identify confounders and whether they are measured.
- Check correlated exposures, shared sources, selection mechanisms, and omitted variables.
- For legal analysis, consider procedural posture, preservation, harmless error, good faith, and record limitations where relevant.
- State likely direction only when justified; otherwise say unknown.
- Propose a concrete sensitivity analysis or evidence-gathering step.

## Output
Bias register with mechanism, likely impact, evidence, and test.

## Fail conditions
Generic bias list with no connection to the actual design or record.
