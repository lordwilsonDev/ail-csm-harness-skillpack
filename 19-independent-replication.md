# Skill 19 — Independent Second-Pass Verification
## Goal
Repeat the highest-risk checks without relying on the first pass's conclusions.

## Procedure
- Reconstruct key calculations from source counts.
- Independently open and read material authorities.
- Re-evaluate the central inference from the evidence ledger, not the draft narrative.
- Recheck each acceptance test and critical red-team finding.
- Record disagreements between passes; do not average them away.
- If no independent reviewer/tool is available, label this as a self-review, not independent verification.

## Output
Replication record, disagreements, and residual risk.

## Fail conditions
Verification claimed without a distinct check or reviewer.
