# Skill 16 — Stakeholder & Impact Review
## Goal
Identify who is affected and what could go wrong if the artifact is acted upon.

## Procedure
- Identify decision-makers, subjects, affected populations, opposing parties, and implementers.
- Consider false positives, false negatives, reputational, operational, legal, and safety consequences.
- Check privacy, fairness, accessibility, and proportionality.
- Distinguish evidence from stakeholder preferences.
- Avoid substituting stakeholder consensus for proof.

## Output
Impact map and safeguards.

## Fail conditions
Material affected parties or foreseeable harms omitted.
