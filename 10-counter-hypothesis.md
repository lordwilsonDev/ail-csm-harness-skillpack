# Skill 10 — Counter-Hypothesis / Counterargument Generator
## Goal
Construct the strongest credible alternative to the leading explanation.

## Procedure
- State the leading claim in its strongest accurate form.
- Generate alternatives that could produce the same observations.
- For legal work, state the opposing party's strongest doctrinal and factual argument.
- Avoid strawmen; cite opposing authorities or evidence.
- Identify observations that would distinguish alternatives.

## Output
Alternative-explanation matrix: alternative | supporting evidence | contradicting evidence | discriminating test.

## Fail conditions
Only weak alternatives considered or counterargument omitted.
