# Skill 14 — Missingness & Uncertainty Register
## Goal
Make unknowns visible and decision-relevant.

## Procedure
- List missing inputs, inaccessible sources, unobserved variables, and unresolved ambiguities.
- Distinguish "not found," "not measured," "not applicable," and "negative."
- Describe how each gap could change the conclusion.
- Quantify uncertainty when the data support it; do not use precision theater.
- Identify the minimum evidence needed to resolve each critical gap.

## Output
Unknowns register: item | why it matters | consequence | resolution path | blocking status.

## Fail conditions
Unknowns hidden, treated as negative, or omitted from conclusion.
