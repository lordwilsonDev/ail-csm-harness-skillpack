# Skill 17 — Recommendation / Relief Proportionality Gate
## Goal
Ensure the requested action is supported by the evidence and authority.

## Procedure
- State the decision, available options, and governing threshold.
- Separate reversible precautionary steps from irreversible or punitive conclusions.
- Match recommendation strength to evidence strength.
- For legal briefs, ensure relief follows from the record, preservation, standard of review, and applicable remedy.
- State what additional evidence would justify stronger action.
- Do not present a policy choice as a scientific or legal fact.

## Output
Action-to-evidence mapping and recommendation gate.

## Fail conditions
Action exceeds the demonstrated basis or relief is procedurally unavailable.
