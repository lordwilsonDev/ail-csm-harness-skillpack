# Skill 18 — Artifact Format & Completeness Audit
## Goal
Ensure the deliverable is the artifact requested, not merely commentary about it.

## Procedure
- Check every requirement against the traceability matrix.
- Verify genre conventions, headings, tables, citations, definitions, and conclusion.
- Remove unsupported claims and internal drafting notes that do not belong in the final artifact.
- Ensure citations are consistently formatted and placed near supported claims.
- Check that limitations are visible without overwhelming the deliverable.

## Output
Completeness checklist and format findings.

## Fail conditions
Missing required section, wrong genre, or unresolved placeholder represented as final.
