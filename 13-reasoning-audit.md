# Skill 13 — Causal / Legal Reasoning Audit
## Goal
Test whether the conclusion follows from the evidence and governing rules.

## Procedure
- Separate facts, inferences, hypotheses, speculation, and normative choices.
- Make each inferential step explicit.
- Check for causal overclaim, correlation/causation errors, burden shifts, and unsupported analogies.
- In law, identify binding versus persuasive authority and apply the actual holding to the facts.
- Do not manufacture a split; compare the same legal question, posture, and material facts.
- Test the conclusion under the strongest contrary interpretation.

## Output
Claim-to-reasoning map and supported conclusion.

## Fail conditions
Conclusion exceeds evidence or authority; legal rule misstates holding.
