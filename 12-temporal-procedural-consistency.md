# Skill 12 — Temporal & Procedural Consistency
## Goal
Check chronology and procedural posture.

## Procedure
- Build a timeline of events, observations, data collection, decisions, and authorities.
- Verify that exposure precedes outcome where causation is claimed.
- For law, track trial ruling, appeal posture, preservation, standard of review, and subsequent history.
- Check whether cited authority existed at the time of conduct and at the time of decision.
- Reconcile timeline across narrative, tables, and citations.

## Output
Timeline and consistency findings.

## Fail conditions
Impossible chronology, wrong procedural posture, or authority used without accounting for later history.
