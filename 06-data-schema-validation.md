# Skill 06 — Data & Schema Validation
## Goal
Determine whether data are fit for the proposed analysis.

## Procedure
- Identify unit of analysis, grain, population, time window, inclusion rules, and denominator.
- Check schema, types, missingness, duplicates, impossible values, and conflicting records.
- Reconcile totals across tables and source files.
- Separate synthetic/demo data from observed data.
- Record transformations and exclusions.

## Output
Data-quality report and validated analysis dataset specification.

## Fail conditions
Unknown grain, irreconcilable totals, or synthetic data presented as observed.
