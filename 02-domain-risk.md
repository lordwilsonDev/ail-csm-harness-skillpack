# Skill 02 — Domain Boundary & Risk Classification
## Goal
Identify domain-specific rules and the consequence of error.

## Procedure
- Name primary and secondary domains; do not transfer standards across domains.
- Identify jurisdiction, population, time period, and decision-maker where relevant.
- Classify error impact: low / material / high / critical.
- Identify professional review, safety, privacy, or legal constraints.
- Select applicable domain adapter and source hierarchy.

## Output
`domain_profile`: boundaries, governing standards, risk tier, required expertise/review.

## Fail conditions
Using a generic method where a domain-specific standard controls.
