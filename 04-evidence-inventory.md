# Skill 04 — Evidence Inventory & Provenance
## Goal
Track all evidence without upgrading assumptions into facts.

## Procedure
- Inventory files, datasets, cases, statutes, studies, interviews, and tool outputs.
- Assign stable evidence IDs and record origin, date, scope, and integrity.
- Classify each item as primary, secondary, user-provided, synthetic, or inferred.
- Record what each item can and cannot establish.
- Preserve exact source passages or data row references where possible.

## Output
Evidence ledger with provenance and limitations.

## Fail conditions
A material claim has no traceable evidence or labeled assumption.
