# Skill 09 — Method & Design Audit
## Goal
Check whether the method can answer the stated question.

## Procedure
- Identify design and estimand.
- Test whether sampling, measurement, and comparison groups support the inference.
- Check assumptions required by the method.
- Compare the chosen method with plausible alternatives and explain tradeoffs.
- Identify what the design cannot establish.

## Output
Method-fit assessment: fit, assumptions, limitations, alternatives.

## Fail conditions
Method does not match data structure or requested inference.
