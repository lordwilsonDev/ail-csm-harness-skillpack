# Skill 07 — Definitions & Inclusion-Rule Audit
## Goal
Ensure definitions are operational, reproducible, and independent of the conclusion.

## Procedure
- Test definitions against positive, negative, and borderline examples.
- Check person/place/time or equivalent scope dimensions.
- Ensure outcome/case criteria do not encode the suspected exposure or desired result.
- Check whether definitions change across groups or time.
- Document sensitivity analyses for plausible alternative definitions.

## Output
Definition audit with edge cases and alternative definitions.

## Fail conditions
Circular definition, ambiguous inclusion, or unexplained group-specific criteria.
