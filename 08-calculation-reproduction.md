# Skill 08 — Calculation & Reproduction Audit
## Goal
Independently reproduce every material numerical result.

## Procedure
- Rebuild calculations from raw counts, not displayed results.
- Show formulas, units, denominators, rounding, and intermediate values.
- Independently compute key results using a second method or script where practical.
- Check boundary cases, zero cells, missing values, and multiple comparisons.
- Verify tables, narrative, and figures agree.
- For epidemiology, validate 2x2 cells, attack rates, RR/OR, confidence intervals, and totals.

## Output
Reproduction sheet and discrepancy log.

## Fail conditions
Any unreconciled arithmetic, unexplained rounding, or denominator mismatch.
