# Skill 05 — Source & Citation Verification
## Goal
Verify existence, identity, currency, and claim-level support for citations.

## Procedure
- Resolve each citation to an authoritative source where possible.
- Verify title, author/court/agency, date, edition, docket/citation, and URL.
- Open the cited source; do not rely on search snippets.
- Match the cited proposition to the source's actual text and context.
- Check subsequent history, amendments, overruling, withdrawal, or supersession.
- Distinguish a source that exists from one that supports the claim.
- Mark inaccessible or unverified citations explicitly; never fabricate pinpoint citations.

## Output
Citation matrix: citation | identity verified | proposition supported | currency/history | pinpoint | status.

## Fail conditions
Fabricated authority, materially misstated holding, dead/unverified source represented as checked.
